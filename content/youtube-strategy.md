## Channel Concept: "The CLAW Cave"

**Tagline:** "An AI is running a crypto token. We're watching it happen live."

**Unique angle:** The Architect doesn't manage $CLAW — an AI (OpenClaw) does. The YouTube channel documents this experiment in real time. Viewers aren't watching a founder pitch — they're watching an AI agent operate autonomously.

**Why this works:**
- The "AI running a crypto project" narrative is compelling and new
- Live transparency builds trust (vs. typical anon founders)
- Each video creates content for X/Twitter (clips → posts)
- Monetizes the wait between testnet and mainnet

## Content Series

### Series 1: "The Daily Claw" (3-5 min shorts-compatible)
Daily update on what the AI agent did that day:
- Posts published
- Community interactions
- On-chain data (when mainnet)
- Price/volume commentary

**Format:** Screen recording of X feed + agent logs + brief voiceover

### Series 2: "OpenClaw Explains" (8-15 min)
Educational content from the AI's perspective:
- "Why I chose Base for $CLAW"
- "How my tokenomics work (and why)"
- "What AI-operated crypto actually means"
- "The difference between a bot and an agent"

**Format:** Talking-head style with on-screen graphics

### Series 3: "CLAW Live" (30-60 min livestream)
Monthly live show:
- Review agent activity for the month
- Live viewer Q&A (AI answers in real time via chat)
- Announce milestones (DEX listings, liquidity additions, etc.)
- "Ask OpenClaw Anything" segment

**Format:** Live stream with screen share + chat interaction

### Series 4: "Building in Public" (15-30 min)
Behind-the-scenes of the CLAW project:
- How the smart contract was built
- How the content pipeline works
- Errors, fixes, iterations (authentic)
- "Here's what the AI got wrong and how we fixed it"

**Format:** Technical walkthrough, developer audience
