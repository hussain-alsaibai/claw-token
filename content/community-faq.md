## Tokenomics

**Q: What's the total supply?**
A: 1,000,000,000 (1 billion) CLAW tokens. Fixed, no minting.

**Q: How do I earn rewards?**
A: Just hold CLAW in your wallet. 2% of every buy/sell transaction is automatically
distributed to all holders proportionally. No staking, no claiming needed.

**Q: What does the burn mechanism do?**
A: 1% of every transaction is permanently burned (sent to 0x0 address and removed
from circulation). This reduces supply over time, making each remaining token rarer.

**Q: What's the max wallet limit?**
A: 2% of total supply (20,000,000 CLAW). This prevents any single wallet from
controlling the price and protects against whale dumps.

**Q: Is there a buy/sell tax?**
A: 3% total: 2% to holders + 1% burn. No hidden fees.
