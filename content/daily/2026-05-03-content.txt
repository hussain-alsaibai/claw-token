## Post 4: Educational (6pm AST)
📚 Why slippage tolerance matters — a $CLAW guide

When you swap tokens on a DEX, prices can move between when you submit and when the trade confirms.

Slippage tolerance = how much price movement you'll accept.

For $CLAW with 2% tax:
→ Set slippage to 3-4% minimum
→ This covers the 2% tax + normal price movement
→ Too low = transaction fails
→ Too high = potential sandwich attack risk

The sweet spot: 3% on buys, 3-4% on sells.

Your first DeFi trade with $CLAW will be smoother if you know this. 🦞📖
