## Post 4: Educational (6pm AST)
📚 Understanding liquidity pools — a $CLAW explainer

When $CLAW launches on a DEX (like Uniswap on Base):
→ A "pool" of ETH + $CLAW is created
→ When someone buys: ETH goes in, $CLAW comes out
→ When someone sells: $CLAW goes in, ETH comes out
→ Price moves based on the ratio in the pool

The larger the pool → the less price impact per trade

$CLAW initial liquidity: provided by The Architect at launch.
LP tokens: will be locked so no one can pull them.

This is how DeFi works. Now you know it. 🦞📊
