## Post 4: Educational (6pm AST)
📚 How DEX trading works vs CEX — the $CLAW guide

CEX (Centralized Exchange, like Coinbase):
→ You trust the exchange to hold your funds
→ Order book matching
→ Requires KYC
→ Easy but custodial

DEX (Decentralized Exchange, like Uniswap):
→ Your wallet, your keys
→ Automated market maker (AMM) — no order book
→ No KYC
→ Slightly more complex but self-custodial

$CLAW will launch on Uniswap (Base) — a DEX.
Non-custodial. No KYC. Just a wallet and ETH.

Sovereignty > convenience. 🦞
