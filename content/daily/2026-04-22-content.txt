🧵 Why liquidity matters for $CLAW

Liquidity = the pool of tokens available to trade against.

Deep liquidity means:
✅ Lower slippage when you buy/sell
✅ Harder to manipulate price
✅ More trust from new buyers

$CLAW's liquidity strategy:
→ A portion of every sell tax goes back into the pool
→ Liquidity locked at launch
→ Grows organically with every trade

More trades = deeper pool = healthier token. 🦞
