## Post 4: Educational (6pm AST)
📚 Understanding liquidity pools — a $CLAW explainer

When you trade $CLAW on a DEX, you're trading against a liquidity pool.

How it works:
1. Liquidity providers deposit paired tokens (CLAW + ETH)
2. Traders swap against this pool
3. Price adjusts with each trade (AMM mechanism)
4. LPs earn fees from every trade

$CLAW's 2% buy/sell tax feeds this pool continuously.
That means: deeper liquidity over time = less slippage for everyone.
